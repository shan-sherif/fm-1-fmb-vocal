Studio

Voice engine based on CHIMERA by Jameson Hodge (MIT licence). Pronunciations from the CMU Pronouncing Dictionary (Carnegie Mellon University, BSD licence); random words from SCOWL (Kevin Atkinson, its notice). Unofficial: not affiliated with M-VAVE, and not endorsed by CHIMERA's author.

Write the words the FM-1 sings: up to eight lines, one per slot, each key the next syllable. Try them on the emulated FM-1 (Play speaks a line; its keys then sing it), then send them to yours over USB.

Words the dictionary does not know

A name or a made-up word is read by spelling rules on the FM-1, which can get it wrong. Say how it sounds with words the dictionary knows (sheh vawn for Siobhan), or in ARPAbet (SH AH0 V AO1 N), in the box under it. A hyphen in the words splits syllables where you want (ti-ger); a lone - holds the vowel onto the next key.
