# The Bestiary's songbird (0.7.0): LFO opens the Bestiary, ALGORITHM picks the songbird (its airway on the second core
# at 2x); a key held plays its two voices (C4 three octaves up: CHIMERA's creatureKeyHz centres its 400..9000 Hz on
# middle C), PLAY its flute phrase, PRESET the canary trill with FX at a key; SEL its throat page; kept through a
# reboot; ALGORITHM back down to the seal; LFO back to the human voice.
#   python base/tools/build.py app --host; app/build/host/fmb-vocal_host app/tests/bird.txt app/build/host
