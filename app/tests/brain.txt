# The brain shows the two cores at work (FMB Vocal): the second core's load its colour (cyan idle, through green and
# yellow, to red at 85 %), the first core's its brightness (35 % idle to full at 70 %); the loads read every half second
# (the GLO page's), the home screen redrawn when either moves 5 %. The simulator measures no loads: "loads C2 C1"
# stands in for them.
#   python base/tools/build.py app --host; app/build/host/fmb-vocal_host app/tests/brain.txt app/build/host
wait 800
# idle: cyan, dim (0x3fd0e0 at 35 %)
loads 0 0
wait 600
expect brainrgb 1460302
shot brain-idle.png
# core 2 at 65 %: yellow; core 1 idle: dim (0xffd23f at 35 %)
loads 65 0
wait 600
expect brainrgb 5851414
# core 2 over 85 %: red; core 1 at 70 % or more: full (0xff4d4d)
loads 90 75
wait 600
expect brainrgb 16731469
shot brain-busy.png
# singing keeps it (the loads are the cores', not the voice's)
key 7 down
wait 600
expect brainrgb 16731469
key 7 up
wait 300
